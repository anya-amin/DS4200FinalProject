Recommendations

Based on our analysis, here are actionable recommendations for hosts and travelers to understand these market trends.

Overall Conclusions

Overall, throughout a lot can be learned from the visualizations about this topic. To begin, it is clear that depending on different characteristics of an Airbnb, price can vary from there. For instance, using the heat map demonstrated how number of bedrooms and bathrooms have a strong correlation with price meanwhile one of the scatterplots showed that host response rate does not really effect price. Therefore, from what has been learned, the physical attributes of the Airbnb including the location and type have more of an effect on the price than something like the host characterisitcs. The D3 map supports this by showing that central neighborhoods like Back Bay and Beacon Hill have higher prices and listing density. The scatter plot also shows that high review scores appear across all price ranges, suggesting value perception isn’t strictly tied to cost. Let's explore more into this!

Recommendations for Airbnb Owners

The data shows that the strongest drivers of nightly price are the number of bedrooms and bathrooms. This means that Airbnb owners can justify higher pricing by how many guests the space can comfortably support. Even a unit with a smaller living area can command a higher price if it offers multiple bedrooms or bathrooms that accommodate larger groups. Owners should capitalize on demand for sleeping capacity, bathroom access, and highlighting these features clearly in their listings. Host responsiveness is a must regardless of the price, therefore it cannot justify higher pricing. The D3 map highlights that listings in high-demand areas can justify premium pricing, so location should be emphasized. The scatter plot also shows that entire homes are priced higher but don’t always score better, so guest experience matters.

Recommendations for Airbnb Consumers

For Airbnb consumers, the visualizations suggest several ways to search more efficiently and save money. First, travelers on a tighter budget should look at private rooms because the box plot shows that they consistently cost much less than entire homes across almost every neighborhood. This makes them a good option for students and solo travelers. Moreover, the scatter plots show that host response rates stay very high for almost all listings. This means guests should expect cheaper listings to have high response rates and not be pressured to pay a premium for that. Finally, consumers should focus more on the physical characteristics of the listing. The heatmap demonstrates that features like bathrooms and bedrooms are more strongly tied to price than reviews or availability. The scatter plot shows that private and shared rooms offer strong value at lower prices. The D3 map also helps identify which neighborhoods offer cheaper listings for budget-conscious travelers. When comparing options, guests who want the best value should weigh layout and amenities more heavily than popularity metrics.

Future Work

In the future, the analysis can be expanded by incorporating additional Airbnb cancellation policies, amenities (Wi-Fi, parking, air conditioning), proximity to tourist spots, square footage, or seasonal demand. It would also be valuable to integrate more geospatial visualizations to explore how price and demand vary block-by-block within neighborhoods. Finally, regression modeling or machine-learning techniques could be applied to formally quantify which factors most strongly predict price. Adding room type filters to the D3 map could help explore spatial trends by listing category. Including seasonal data in scatter plots could also reveal how price and value shift throughout the year. These extensions would lead to a more comprehensive understanding of what drives pricing in short-term rental markets. With that all being said, there are different price determinants for Airbnbs, and while this was a starting point to analyzing these factors, more can be explored in the coming years as the market grows.
